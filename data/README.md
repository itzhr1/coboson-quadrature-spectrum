# Data files

All files are JSON. Unless stated otherwise, the scale is `r = 1`, `b = pi/2`, the initial state is the vacuum, the bin convention at an exact bin edge is `F = +1` (`tie = 1`), and probabilities are fractions (not percent). "Archived" files are the outputs of the long scans; they are byte-identical to the files distributed with revisions v5–v7 and are not regenerated by `reproduce.sh`. "Fresh" files are regenerated by `python code/revision_checks.py [--extended]`.

Common field names: `S` Schmidt rank (number of retained modes); `tie` bin-edge convention (`+1`/`-1`); `dD` displacement (Weyl) defect `delta_D`; `eps` binned anticommutation defect `||{X,Z}|0>||`; `praw` success probability of the raw sequential protocol; `X0`, `Z0` vacuum expectations of the binned quadratures; `t` accepted weight `<0|E|0>`; `mx`, `mz` vacuum expectations of the corrected observables `X_E`, `Z~` (`my = 0` for real vacua); `p` corrected-protocol singlet probability from two vacua; `bloch` Bloch length `|m|/t` of the filtered reduced qubit; `naz`, `nax` number of `Q_w`, `Q_r` eigenvalues exactly on a bin edge; `edge_mass_*` vacuum weight on them; `margin_*` distance of the spectrum from the nearest bin edge; `cutoff_distance`, `cutoff_near_count` distance of the `|K|` spectrum from the cutoff and the number of eigenvalues within the roundoff band; `method` `dense` or `lanczos`.
